Terms used on this page

Landed means merged to main. Landed share divides those merges by all write tasks, including failed attempts. Low sample means fewer than 10 observations in the relevant denominator.

An interval is the 95% Wilson interval for a share. Wide intervals signal uncertainty, and overlapping intervals alone cannot settle a comparison.

A first-round approval means the first reviewer approved the PR. P0–P2 are findings from the highest priority through P2. A dash means the records lack a value for that metric.
